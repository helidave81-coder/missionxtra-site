Guides

Get airborne with MissionXtra

Short, practical walkthroughs for the workflows crews use every shift.

GUIDE 01

Your first weight & balance

  1. Open Setup and confirm the tail and fitted SUPP kits.
  2. Pick a configuration on the Configuration tab.
  3. Tap a seat and enter a weight, or drag a crew card on.
  4. Add fuel on the Fuel tab and read the live verdict.
  5. Confirm the badge shows Within Limits.
GUIDE 02

Plan a multi-leg mission

  1. Open Mission Manager → Quick Plan.
  2. Add waypoints by ICAO or lat/long.
  3. Or AirDrop an OzRunways .ozpln file to import.
  4. Check per-leg weather on the Flight Plan tab.
  5. Review endurance and reserves for the whole route.
GUIDE 03

Run performance for the site

  1. Tap Full Breakdown to open Performance.
  2. Select the take-off procedure (Vertical, Confined…).
  3. Enter site elevation and obstacles.
  4. Drag the Rotate/TDP slider until the path is green.
  5. Cross-check figures in the RFM chart browser.
GUIDE 04

Assess risk & sign off

  1. Open the Risk Assessment tool.
  2. Score Crew, Weather, Mission, Aircraft & Environment.
  3. Note the LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH band and authority.
  4. Open Load Manifest and capture the PIC signature.
  5. Share the signed A4 PDF to dispatch.
GUIDE 05 · OPERATORS

Publish data to your fleet

  1. In the Mission Editor, edit the tail or config.
  2. Sign off & lock with the weighing report.
  3. Clear the flight-ready pre-publish gate.
  4. Click Publish to Fleet with a change note.
  5. Pilots receive the new versioned data automatically.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is MissionXtra a certified EFB or FMS?
No. MissionXtra is an operational support tool and planning aid. It is not certified by CASA, EASA, FAA or any other aviation regulator as a primary flight-planning system, Electronic Flight Bag (EFB), Flight Management System (FMS), navigation system or aircraft performance-approval tool. It does not replace the approved Rotorcraft Flight Manual — the operator and Pilot in Command remain solely responsible for independently verifying all data, loading, performance and operational decisions. Every screen and manifest states this clearly.
Which aircraft are supported?
The AW139 is the full performance type — weight & balance plus the complete RFM performance suite. The AW109, AS350, B412 and BK117 are supported for weight & balance and MTOW. We can integrate additional types on request.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Synced fleet data, the terrain/elevation grid and the airport database are bundled on the device, and your operator branding shows offline. Live weather needs a connection, but the core W&B and performance work without one.
How do pilots get the right data?
Operators author and sign off their data in the macOS Mission Editor or the secure web portal, then publish it over-the-air. Each pilot's iPad receives the latest versioned, checksummed data for the types they're assigned — with a status pill and a stale-data warning so they always know what they're flying.
How is our data kept separate and secure?
Each operator's data is isolated at the backend with row-level security, admin keys are held only in the Mac Keychain, the web portal is protected with two-factor authentication, and an append-only audit log records who changed, signed off, suspended or published what — exportable to CSV for your compliance file.
Who is it built for?
Built by operational helicopter pilots, with a focus on EMS/HEMS, SAR/winch and offshore operators — though it suits any helicopter operator running a supported type. Hoist-aware envelopes, inter-hospital and primary-HEMS mission types, patient configs and the built-in risk assessment all reflect real mission-critical operations.
