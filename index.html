Guides

Get airborne with MissionXtra

Short, practical walkthroughs for the workflows crews use every shift.

GUIDE 01

Your first weight & balance

  1. Open Setup and confirm the tail and fitted SUPP kits.
  2. Pick a configuration on the Configuration tab.
  3. On Crew & Pax, add today's shift crew with one tap.
  4. Add fuel on the Fuel tab and read the live verdict.
  5. Confirm the badge shows Within Limits.
GUIDE 02

Plan a multi-leg mission

  1. Open Mission Manager → Quick Plan.
  2. Add waypoints by ICAO or lat/long.
  3. Or AirDrop an OzRunways .ozpln file to import.
  4. Check per-leg weather on the Flight Plan tab.
  5. Review endurance and reserves for the whole route.
GUIDE 03

Run performance for the site

  1. Tap Full Breakdown + CG to open Performance.
  2. Select the take-off procedure (Vertical, Confined…).
  3. Enter site elevation and obstacles — or let the HLS Register fill them.
  4. Drag the Rotate / TDP slider until the path is green.
  5. Switch to Obstacle Profile to confirm clearance.
GUIDE 04

Sign off & close the mission

  1. Open Load Manifest and review the item table.
  2. Capture the PIC signature on glass.
  3. Share the signed A4 PDF to dispatch.
  4. Use Share Flight to AirDrop the flight to another crew.
  5. After the last leg, open the Flight report.
GUIDE 05 · OPERATORS

Roster crews & publish

  1. In the Mission Editor, open Shift Roster.
  2. Drag crew onto day and night shifts, or set a weekly pattern.
  3. Click Publish Roster to push it to your iPads.
  4. Edit tails or configs, then Publish the fleet data.
  5. Check Tools → Live on pilots' iPads to confirm they're in sync.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is MissionXtra a certified EFB or FMS?
No. MissionXtra is an operational support tool and planning aid. It is not certified by CASA, EASA, FAA or any other aviation regulator as a primary flight-planning system, Electronic Flight Bag (EFB), Flight Management System (FMS), navigation system or aircraft performance-approval tool. It does not replace the approved Rotorcraft Flight Manual — the operator and Pilot in Command remain solely responsible for independently verifying all data, loading, performance and operational decisions. Every screen and manifest states this clearly.
Which aircraft are supported?
The AW139 is the full performance type — weight & balance plus the complete RFM performance suite. The AW109, AS350, B412 and BK117 are supported for weight & balance and MTOW. We can integrate additional types on request.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Synced fleet data, the terrain/elevation grid and the airport database are bundled on the device, and your operator branding shows offline. Live weather needs a connection, but the core W&B and performance work without one.
How do I get the two apps?
The iPad app is downloaded from the Apple App Store, so any pilot can install it and sign in with the credentials their operator issues. The Mission Editor for Mac is not published to any app store — licensed operators download it from their MissionXtra client portal after signing in. It's signed and notarised by Apple, runs on Apple silicon Macs, and updates itself automatically with each release verified by cryptographic signature.
How do pilots get the right data?
Operators author and sign off their data in the Mission Editor, then publish it over-the-air. Each pilot's iPad receives the latest versioned, checksummed data for the types they're assigned — with a status pill and a stale-data warning so they always know what they're flying. Operators can confirm at a glance via Tools → Live on pilots' iPads.
What is the HLS Register?
It's your operator's own database of surveyed helicopter landing sites — hospital pads, ALAs and confined sites — recording each one's elevation, limits, contacts, photos and the controlling obstacles on every surveyed approach and departure path. When a pilot's plan lands at a registered site, the iPad pre-fills the performance page from your survey, clearly badged as operator survey data and overridable in one tap. It is advisory information supplied by your organisation, not certified data.
How is our data kept separate and secure?
Each operator's data is isolated at the backend with row-level security, admin keys are held only in the Mac Keychain, the web portal is protected with two-factor authentication, and an append-only audit log records who changed, signed off, suspended or published what — exportable to CSV for your compliance file.
Who is it built for?
Built by operational helicopter pilots, with a focus on EMS/HEMS, SAR/winch and offshore operators — though it suits any helicopter operator running a supported type. Hoist-aware envelopes, stretcher and patient configurations, per-base day/night rostering and surveyed landing-site data all reflect real mission-critical operations.

Two apps. One source of truth.

The iPad app comes from the App Store, so any pilot can install it. The Mission Editor is issued to operators only — you download it from your MissionXtra client portal after signing in.
